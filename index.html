springday poster

springday poster is a small personal tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It lets the creator of springday.tokyo prepare a finished short video (a Reel or Short), write its caption, choose when it goes out, and send it to her own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What it does

  • Shows the creator's finished videos from a folder on her own computer.
  • Lets her write a caption, choose who can see the post, and pick a date and time.
  • After she confirms, sends the video and caption to the platform she chose, using that platform's official API.

Who uses it

At the moment the app is used by one person: its creator, on her own accounts. It does not collect information from anyone else, and it does not run on any server of ours.

Platforms

The app connects to Instagram (Meta), TikTok and YouTube only when the account owner signs in and approves access on that platform's own screen. Access can be removed at any time. See the Privacy Policy.
