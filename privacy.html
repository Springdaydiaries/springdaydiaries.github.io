Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

springday poster ("the app") is a personal tool made and used by springday.diaries, the creator of springday.tokyo. Contact: springday.diaries@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

  • Sign-in tokens that Instagram, TikTok or YouTube give the app when the account owner approves access.
  • Basic account details needed to post: the account's username or channel name, and, for TikTok, the account's posting options (such as which privacy settings are available).
  • The videos and captions the account owner chooses to post, and the date and time she chooses.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the account owner's own computer, in a private folder. We do not run any server that receives or stores it. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and sets no cookies for tracking.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share this information. The only time a video, caption or token leaves the computer is when the account owner confirms a post: it is then sent directly to the platform she chose (Meta for Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube/Google) so that it can be published.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app with YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The app only uses YouTube access to upload videos the account owner chooses to her own channel.

TikTok

The app uses TikTok's Content Posting API to publish videos the account owner chooses to her own TikTok account. Access can be removed in the TikTok app under the account's connected apps and services settings.

Instagram

The app uses Meta's Instagram API to publish Reels the account owner chooses to her own Instagram account. Access can be removed in the Instagram or Facebook settings under connected apps.

Deleting your data

Remove the app's access on each platform (links above), then delete the app's folder on your computer. That removes all stored tokens, captions and the post list. Nothing remains with us, because we hold nothing.

Children

The app is a tool for an adult account owner and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
